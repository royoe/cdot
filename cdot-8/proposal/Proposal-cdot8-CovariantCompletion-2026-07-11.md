## 0. Charter, and the relationship to cdot-7

cdot-8 is **not a supersession** (unlike cdot-6→cdot-7, which replaced a broken local
closure). It is a *completion program*: a generally covariant theory whose
preferred-frame limit **is** cdot-7 — same census closure, same $a_0=\lambda\dot c$
portal, same fitted phenomenology — and which additionally makes relativistic-level
predictions (lensing, PPN, gravitational waves) that cdot-7's Newtonian scope (§6
item 7) cannot. Two exit states, both acceptable:

- **Success**: cdot-8 passes the gates of §7 and reduces to cdot-7's empirical layer;
  it then absorbs cdot-7 the way cdot-7's §2.2 absorbed the fixed-point closure — as
  the special case that survives.
- **Failure**: a kill condition in §6 triggers; the negative result is documented to
  this project's usual standard, item 7 is re-marked "attempted, obstructed at [WP],"
  and cdot-7 stands unaffected.

cdot-7's own priority queue (items 1–3, 5–6, 10–11 — above all the **KATRIN clock**,
which runs on publication timescales) continues in parallel and is not to be starved
by cdot-8 work. Relation to item 12 (the deferred classical-frame presentation): that
deferral was about *publication packaging* of the same physics; this charter is about
*building new physics*. The 2026-07-08 "own terms first" decision is amended, not
reversed: cdot-8 **is** the framework's own terms — the static frame survives as the
theory's preferred foliation, now with dynamical citizenship.

## 1. Design constraints inherited as negative results

These are cdot-6/7's paid-for lessons, promoted to cdot-8 axioms of *avoidance*:

**D1 — No single-scalar universal dictionary.** The recorded PV/Atkinson obstruction,
stated exactly: the spatial (potential-driven) and temporal (cosmological) variations
of $c$ require *different* dictionaries —

| | spatial job (match GR tests; PV convention) | temporal job (cdot-7, forced by LLR + $\dot G/G$ + data) |
|---|---|---|
| mass | $m\propto c^{-3/2}$ | $m\propto c^{+1/2}$ |
| length unit | $\propto c^{+1/2}$ | $\propto c^{-3/2}$ |
| frequency unit | $\propto c^{+1}$ | $\propto c^{+5/2}$ |
| $\alpha_G$ | $\propto c^{-4}$ (non-invariant, *by design* — it encodes time dilation/bending) | invariant (Planck-unit invariance, *by design* — it passes LLR/clock bounds) |

A single scalar with universal couplings forces the two columns equal; contradiction;
cdot-6 died there. cdot-8 therefore separates the jobs structurally: **local
relativistic phenomenology is carried by the tensor metric, not by a spatially
varying $c$**; "variable $c$" survives only as the cosmological, foliation-defined
relation between epochs. Nothing in cdot-8 is permitted to reintroduce a PV-style
spatial $c(x)$ dictionary.

**D2 — No potential-based MOND.** cdot-6's categorical result: any local closure
built on a potential-like scalar gives $1/r^2$ asymptotics, for any smooth
combination. Hence the completion target is **relativistic AQUAL** — GR-like PPN
where GR is tested, MOND in the weak field — never GR itself. "GR-compatible" in
cdot-8 means *recovers GR where GR is measured*, nothing stronger.

**D3 — $c_\text{gw}=c_\gamma$ to $10^{-15}$** (GW170817). Non-negotiable; must hold
by construction, not by tuning.

**D4 — Matter-sector Lorentz invariance and EP.** Foliation effects confined to the
gravity sector; matter universally coupled. (This is not a constraint *on top of*
cdot-7's premises — it *is* premise 3, see K1.)

covariant language ("all dimensionless local physics epoch-invariant; one portal"),
and it is simultaneously D4. If cdot-8 works, **item 4's mechanism debt is
discharged**: Planck-unit invariance stops being a postulated symmetry and becomes
the definition of a metric theory with universal coupling — and since the 07-11
census update, the counting law rides on the same statement. The $(g,s)=(0,\tfrac12)$
exponents stop being adopted and become frame-map bookkeeping (K5).

integral over the aether-orthogonal slices (WP2) — the genuinely novel object.

framework's distinctive, data-confronted claim ($\hat a_0(z)$, §5.5) — the thing
cdot-8 exists to covariantize, not to replace.

**K4 — The entire empirical layer, as acceptance data.** The four-term fit
($\delta_0=-0.0909$, $\kappa\lambda=0.4355$, $\lambda=0.3056$,
$\Sigma m_\nu=1.374$ eV, $q_0=-0.44$, age 12.9 Gyr); the exact EdS photon-sector
correspondence; the instability-as-$\Lambda$ separatrix with $\delta_0<0$ forced by
global regularity; the radiation-era trajectory (fixed points 1.72/3.44,
$x(1100)=2.61$); the seed-criterion structure (both asymptotes backward-attracting).
cdot-8 must reproduce these in its preferred-frame limit — they are its gate G4, and

**K5 — The coordinate↔local dictionary and redshift law** ($1+z=(c_0/c)^{3/2}$,
density map $c^p\to c^{p-7}$) — to be *re-derived* in cdot-8 as the map between
aether-frame components and $\hat g$-proper quantities (WP1). Until re-derived, used
but flagged.

**K6 — Methodology.** Adopted-vs-derived flagging at point of introduction;
verify-then-trust for every external anchor and every inherited number; per-prompt

**Demoted, explicitly:** premise 1's literal "nothing curves" becomes a statement

## 3. The AeST connection: chassis, resonances, and one critical divergence

**The chassis.** Skordis & Złośnik's Aether-Scalar-Tensor theory (AeST; *PRL* 127,
161302 (2021), arXiv:2007.00082): metric $g_{\mu\nu}$, unit-timelike vector $A_\mu$,
scalar $\phi$; constructed so the tensor mode propagates at $c_\text{EM}$ **in all
situations** (D3 by construction); MOND quasistatic limit with correct galactic
lensing (gate G1 solved in the literature, not by us); linear-stability and
Hamiltonian analyses exist (§4, WP0 findings). AeST is adopted as the *starting
chassis* — field content and quasistatic/GW structure — not as a finished answer.

**Structural resonances (why this chassis and not another):**
- AeST's scalar sector behaves as shift-symmetric k-essence, which **spontaneously
  breaks time diffeomorphisms** (ghost-condensate-like), generating an emergent
  preferred foliation. cdot-7's coordinate frame — until now a philosophical choice —
  acquires dynamical citizenship for free: **M1: coordinate time ↔ the scalar clock
  of the broken phase; static Euclidean slices ↔ aether-orthogonal slices.**
- The aether frame supplies exactly the simultaneity slicing the census integral
  needs (M4 below): the census was always a foliation-dependent object; AeST provides
  the foliation as a field.
- In AeST, $a_0$ is a *parameter* assembled from the free function's normalization
  and the scalar's cosmological velocity — leaving the $a_0\sim cH_0$ coincidence
  unexplained. That parameter is precisely what cdot-8 exists to promote to a
  **dynamical output**: **M2: $Q\equiv A^\mu\nabla_\mu\phi$ as the carrier of
  $\dot c$; $a_0=\lambda\dot c$ ↔ $a_0$(free-function norm, $Q_0$), with $Q_0(t)$
  fixed by the census closure** rather than chosen.

**The critical divergence, stated up front because it is the largest risk.** AeST's
cosmological success rests on its scalar's energy density evolving **dust-like,
$\propto(1+z)^3$** — i.e., the scalar *is* the dark-matter mimicker, and the CMB/
matter-power fits in the AeST literature are fits *of that component*. cdot-7's
central claim is the opposite: $\Omega_\text{closure}=0.074\approx\Omega_b+\Omega_\nu$,
no dark component, with the expansion history's late-time departure coming from the
separatrix instability, not from $\Lambda$ or a scalar fluid. **cdot-8 therefore
keeps AeST's field content, quasistatic limit, and GW sector, but must replace its
cosmological branch wholesale with the census-closed one.** Consequences accepted
knowingly: (i) the AeST literature's CMB success does *not* transfer — cdot-8's CMB
story remains hostage to items 5–6 exactly as cdot-7's is; (ii) whether the field
equations even admit a census-closed branch is an open question — it is WP3, the
make-or-break work package.

**WP0 first pass — AeST status, verified by search this session (2026-07-11), to be
re-run at WP0 proper:**
- Minkowski linear stability: healthy propagating massive vector/scalar modes given
  parameter constraints (Skordis & Złośnik, *PRD* 106, 104041 (2022)); one
  non-propagating mode with unbounded Hamiltonian for $k<\mu$, argued Jeans-like and
  benign — a condition cdot-8 must re-examine on its *own* cosmological branch, since
  the benign-ness argument was made on theirs.
- Full nonperturbative Hamiltonian: six physical degrees of freedom, 4 first-class +
  4 second-class constraints (Bataki, Skordis & Złośnik, *PRD* 110, 044015 (2024)) —
  the constraint algebra WP3 must be checked against.
- Quasistatic structure: **two** quasistatic limits (Mistele, arXiv:2305.07742); the
  potential acquires a mass $\mu$ from the broken phase, so the static behavior
  *departs from MOND beyond $r\sim\mu^{-1}$* (Verwayen, Skordis & Bœhm, *MNRAS* 531,
  272 (2024)) — a feature, not only a bug: a possible handle on MOND's cluster-scale
  residual (cdot-7 §5.6 option (iv)), flagged for later, not relied on.
- Direct data contact exists at small accelerations: AeST vs. weak-lensing RAR
  (Mistele, McGaugh & Hossenfelder, *A&A* 676, A100 (2023)) — the natural literature
  interface for K3/K4's lensing-RAR-by-redshift channel (item 1's blocked secondary
  channel, unblocked by cdot-8 if G1 passes with *evolving* $a_0$).
- Cosmological pathologies possible: sudden singularities in AeST cosmology (Rosa &
  Złośnik, *PRD* 109, 104077 (2024)) — to be checked against, and possibly
  *identified with*, cdot-7's own finite-coordinate-time structures (the $\delta_0>0$
  breakdown; the future runaway) — an intriguing echo, flagged as a check, not a claim.
- Strong-field solutions exist (stealth black holes, Skordis & Vokrouhlický, *JCAP*
  03, 035 (2025); slowly rotating neutron stars, arXiv:2505.03527) with binary-pulsar
  constraints explicitly flagged open in that literature — G3's hardest sub-gate.

1. The covariant census (M4) and its closure constraint (M5) — a global,
   foliation-dependent Machian condition imposed on local field equations.
   Quasi-local Mach conditions are notoriously delicate; this is the research
   program's heart.
2. Dynamical $a_0(z)$ from that closure — every relativistic MOND theory to date has
   constant-parameter $a_0$; cdot-7's fitted $\hat a_0(z)$ evolution is the
   discriminating observable cdot-8 must predict and the literature cannot.

   currently gets late-time acceleration the ordinary way; cdot-8 replaces that with
   the separatrix.
4. The census radiation era (two fixed points, $x(z_\text{recomb})=2.61$) as the
   covariant theory's early background — no AeST-family cosmology has an analog.

## 8. Risk register

(i) **Chassis risk**: AeST's open problems (the $k<\mu$ mode on our branch; the
two-quasistatic-limit subtleties) may be fatal — WP0 full pass first. (ii) **Census
risk**: WP3's kill condition is real; global constraints on local field equations
often fail consistency. (iii) **Deflation risk, faced not avoided**: AeST-type
dynamics might explain $a_0\sim cH_0$ *without* a Machian closure, deflating cdot-7's
distinctive claim — the discriminator is $\hat a_0(z)$'s *shape*, which the census
closure predicts and a parameter-$a_0$ theory does not; keep that comparison explicit
from WP5 onward. (iv) **Opportunity cost**: cdot-7's item 1–2 program (MCMC, RAR
covariance, $H_0$; the KATRIN clock) must not stall — recommend cdot-8 work proceed
only in sessions that would not otherwise advance those. (v) **Ontology cost**: the
demotion of literal flatness (§2) is a real philosophical concession, made knowingly
and recorded here, consistent with §1's own framing and amending (not reversing) the
item-12 decision.
