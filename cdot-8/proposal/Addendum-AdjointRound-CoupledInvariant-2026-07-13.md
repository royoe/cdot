The mirrored-equations derivation holds **at fixed background**. But M5's whole
content is that the background is not fixed independently of the census: the
closure ties $Q$ (hence the lapse, hence the trajectory, hence every $g_i$) to
$\mathcal N_\text{tot}$. Once the action is closed over dynamical variables and the
$\delta g$ back-reaction terms are in place, the state–costate pairs couple, and
the per-species product is the **diagonal case** of the true conserved object —
the symplectic pairing of the full linearized system. Audit item 2's "if species
resolution introduces $g_i(\mathcal N)$" therefore understates its own scope: the
$\mathcal N$-dependence is not a possible artifact of the FD interpolation, it is
built into the closure loop itself (fourth-round addendum, flag 4).

The structural expectation remains benign — state–costate linearizations carry
Hamiltonian structure, eigenvalues pair as $\pm\lambda$, and the pairing is what
adjoint systems conserve — so the likely outcome of the honest check is the same
conclusion on a sturdier foundation. But it must be a check, not an inheritance:
