## 2. The target, in three equivalent forms

From your own §1 ingredients — unitary gauge $Q=1/N$, the demanded current
$a^3F_Q\propto N$ (stand-in advisory §4(iii)), and AeST's definitions
$\rho_\phi=-\tfrac{1}{8\pi\tilde G}(F-QF_Q)$, $p_\phi=\tfrac{1}{8\pi\tilde G}F$,
whence $\rho_\phi+p_\phi=\tfrac{1}{8\pi\tilde G}QF_Q$:

$$\text{(i)}\quad a^3F_Q\propto N
\qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad
\text{(ii)}\quad a^3(\rho_\phi+p_\phi)=\text{const}
\qquad\Longleftrightarrow\qquad
\text{(iii)}\quad \dot\rho_\phi+3H(\rho_\phi+p_\phi)=-\dot p_\phi.$$

Derivation: (i)$\times Q$: $a^3\,QF_Q\propto NQ=1$, which is (ii); differentiating
(ii) gives (iii). Consistency with your §2 identity (a check, not an assumption):
your right-hand side $\tfrac{Q}{8\pi\tilde G a^3}\tfrac{d}{dt}(a^3F_Q)$, evaluated
on $a^3F_Q=k/Q$, is $-\tfrac{1}{8\pi\tilde G}F_Q\dot Q=-\dot p_\phi$ — the demanded
source term drops out of your own identity exactly.

Three remarks, in increasing order of usefulness:

- **(ii) is clock-free** — no dot, no $H$, no choice of $t$ vs $\hat\tau$ — and
  exact on *any* trajectory (both sides tied to $c$ exactly), not only the fixed
  point. It is the form to carry through frame-sensitive manipulations.
- **(ii) is the evocative form:** the census-constrained scalar must
  conserve-and-dilute its *inertial* density $\rho+p$ exactly like a conserved
  comoving number of "inertia quanta" — which is to say, like a census. That the
  Machian constraint's imprint on the scalar sector is a counting law is either a
  deep consistency or a very persistent pun; either way it is the right shape.
- **(iii) is the razor:** the multiplier/boundary term's contribution to the
  scalar-sector continuity equation must equal $-\dot p_\phi$ — no adjustable
  function, up to the standing additive-$CQ$ gauge caveat. This supersedes the
  zero-freedom criterion's previous statement (which constrained the *current*)
  with an equivalent statement that constrains the *construction's output* directly,
  and it is the success criterion to test the built term against.

$$\frac{-\dot p_\phi}{\rho_\phi+p_\phi}=\frac{3H_{\hat\tau}p_\phi}{\tfrac95p_\phi}

the candidate channel is the right size (the construction is not chasing orders of
magnitude), yet a bare sweep-rate coupling does not reproduce the demand as-is.
Either the multiplier structure supplies the $10/9$ weighting naturally, or sweep is
not the whole channel. (Noted without weight: $9/10$ is also the fixed-point
$\hat a_0$–$Q$ exponent; whether that is the same $9/10$ or an exponent coincidence

1. **Matter-sector continuity is inviolable — a named constraint from the start.**
   The exchange must be with the gravity/aether/multiplier sector, never with
   matter: K1's universal coupling is what protects LLR, EP, and clock-drift
   bounds, and a matter-sourced exchange resurrects exactly the exposure
   Planck-unit invariance exists to kill. **Any construction that balances the
   ledger by de-conserving matter in $\hat g$ is a kill of that construction** —
   not of WP3, but of that attempt — and should be reported as such immediately
   rather than patched.
2. **The exchange term meets the zero-crossing — check jointly, not sequentially.**
   Form (iii)'s source is $-\dot p_\phi$, and $p_\phi$ evolves through the same
   census-crossover era where the scalar sector's effective density changes sign
   (assessment addendum, flag 2). Run sub-directive 1b's stability check and the
   built exchange term *together* in that era; a term that is healthy on both
   plateaus can still misbehave at the crossing.
3. **WP2 is now a hard prerequisite, not a parallel item.** The census evolution
   equation is the ledger the *total* continuity check balances against; the
   shell-sweep term's precise coefficient lives there. Finalize WP2's
   $\dot{\mathcal N}/\mathcal N$ decomposition before varying the multiplier term.
4. **Flag 3 stands.** The §1 reduction is internally coherent, but write the
   aether-normalization named-assumption line *before* the multiplier term is
   varied — that is where the convention bites, and if the natural convention
   shifts the lapse power in (i), that is a finding to report, not an error to
   absorb.
5. **Success criterion, restated for the record:** the built term passes iff its
   contribution to the scalar-sector continuity equation equals $-\dot p_\phi$
   (equivalently: the total system conserves with $a^3(\rho_\phi+p_\phi)$ constant),
   with no adjustable function up to the additive-$CQ$ gauge piece, while matter
