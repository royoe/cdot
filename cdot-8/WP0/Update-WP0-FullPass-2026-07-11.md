## Part A — The proposal read against the full record, not only cdot-6/7

The proposal's §1 (D1–D4) states its inherited lessons entirely in terms of cdot-6/7.
Reading cdot-1 through cdot-5 directly (not merely cdot-7's own back-references) shows
D1's "two dictionaries" obstruction is not a single lesson from one stage — it is the
**same structural failure, independently rediscovered at least five times**, under five
different local names, across the project's entire history:

| Stage | Local name | The two jobs that couldn't be reconciled |
|---|---|---|
| cdot-2 (OP-9) | conformal-projection Branch A/B | matching FLRW exactly (Branch B) vs. keeping the model's distinctive rival content (Branch A) |
| cdot-2/3/4 (T8) | PV's paired $G\propto c^{-2}$, $m\propto c^{-3/2}$ | $G$'s cosmological law vs. mass's cosmological law — LLR forces invariant mass first (from the Hubble diagram), which then strips $G$'s own PV justification |
| cdot-4 (T22) | the "Two-Regime Dictionary" vs. the river | local PPN-matching mass dressing vs. cosmologically invariant mass — a literal uniqueness theorem showing these are two branches of one system, not two independent options |
| cdot-4 (T23) | the $D_H=dD_p/dz$ "lock" | the redshift clock vs. the distance ruler — one scalar $c(t)$ cannot fix both |
| cdot-6 (LLR floor + MOND no-go) | — | LLR-safety (temporal) vs. producing MOND from a spatial gradient/potential |
| cdot-8 (D1) | the exponent table | spatial (PV-convention) vs. temporal (cdot-7-forced) scalings for mass, length, frequency, $\alpha_G$ |

This is worth stating in the proposal itself, not left implicit: **D1 is not one
cautionary tale, it is the fifth independent confirmation of the same theorem-shaped
obstruction**, found by five different routes (a reciprocity-theorem test, a
ranging-precision test, a PPN uniqueness argument, a BAO shape test, and an exact
potential-closure argument). That convergence is stronger evidence that the obstruction
is real and structural — not an artifact of any one formulation — than any single
instance is, and it is exactly why cdot-8's response (split the jobs structurally across
a tensor metric and a foliation-defined cosmological relation, rather than search for a
cleverer single scalar) is the right shape of fix, not merely *a* fix. Recommend adding
this table (or a condensed form of it) to Proposal §1, with the framing above.

**Two further points the historical record makes and D1–D4 do not capture:**

**(A.1) The project's own named anti-pattern — "selected because it works, not
derived."** T8 flags this explicitly for cdot-3's $G\propto c^{-2}$; cdot-6/ResearchNotes
§7.2 catches itself doing it again with $s=+1/2$ ("its only property is that it cancels

D1/D2, and it bears directly on cdot-8: **M2's stated hope is to derive $\lambda$ from an
action parameter rather than fit it** — good instinct, but the proposal should commit
explicitly to the discipline the project has twice needed to invoke on itself: a
derivation that produces the right number *and no other candidate value* is required;
one that merely reproduces $0.3056$ by construction is the same move T8 and cdot-6 both
flagged as non-progress. Recommend folding this into K6 as an explicit item, not leaving

**(A.2) Two concrete pieces of prior-session experience that bear directly on cdot-8's
gates, not referenced in the proposal.** (i) cdot-2's Etherington-reciprocity test — used

computed, once, the exact kind of solar-system/ephemeris confrontation this WP0 pass

### B.2 Beyond the first pass — what a full search adds

**No fatal pathology found (2024–2026).** No paper — from the Skordis/Złośnik group or
outside it — declares AeST ruled out, ghost-unstable without escape, or devoid of viable
parameter space. What exists is a set of specific, non-fatal, independently-reported
tensions and self-acknowledged conditional results:

- **Weak-lensing tension** (Mistele, McGaugh & Hossenfelder 2023, above): a genuine,
  adversarial, non-Skordis-authored test finding AeST predicts a deviation from MOND at
  low accelerations that the data do not show. Authors' own words: *"This creates a
  tension with the data,"* but stop short of exclusion, calling for "a more advanced
  data analysis... and an improved theoretical understanding."
- **The quasistatic-limit subtlety is a genuine correction, not a footnote.** Mistele
  (2305.07742) shows the standard AeST galactic phenomenology's assumption that the
  aether vector vanishes in the quasistatic limit "is not always justified" — a real gap
  in the papers the proposal otherwise leans on, with percent-level consequences
  generally but larger ones specifically for wide-binary tests (the proposal's own item
  1, "lensing-RAR-by-lens-redshift," should be checked against which of the two limits
  it implicitly assumes).
- **Stability is conditional, not automatic.** The Hamiltonian formalism paper (PRD 110,
  044015) states healthy propagation holds "provided certain constraints on theory
  parameters are satisfied" — no independent group has published a claim that the
  required region is empty or in conflict with the galactic/cosmological viable window,
  but this has not been independently stress-tested either.
- **Galaxy-cluster RAR is non-monotonic** (Durakovic & Skordis, arXiv:2312.00889) — an
  unusual prediction (a peak then a drop mimicking negative mass at very low
  acceleration) presented by the authors as a potentially useful feature, not yet given
  "a full quantitative comparison with observations."
- **The strong-field sector (stealth BHs, neutron stars) is new and not yet
  independently stress-tested** — the papers establishing these solutions (2024–2025)
  explicitly frame them as now *enabling* strong-field tests, not as having passed them.

**Binary-pulsar sector: confirmed still fully open, as the proposal itself flagged.**
No paper — searched exhaustively across AeST, TeVeS, and "aether-scalar-tensor" phrasing
— applies binary-pulsar timing to AeST specifically. This is not a retrieval artifact:
the parallel Einstein-æther/khronometric pulsar-constraint literature is mature and
active through 2026 (Yagi, Blas, Barausse & Yunes 2014, arXiv:1311.7144; Gupta et al.
2021, arXiv:2104.04596; Vaglio et al. 2026, arXiv:2605.01436, the most recent and most
stringent single-system bound, on PSR J1738+0333) — but none of these papers name or map
their bounds onto AeST's parameter space, and no AeST paper cites this machinery. **This
is a concrete, actionable lead for WP6**: the sensitivity-function/orbital-decay
machinery these papers built for Einstein-æther is structurally reusable for AeST's own
constrained aether vector, since both are unit-timelike sigma-model fields in the same
technical family (§B.3 below) — adapting it, rather than starting from scratch, is the
plausible path to closing this gate.

**A genuinely new gap, not previously flagged: no Cassini/ephemeris-level test of
AeST's interpolating function exists.** The classic Hees et al. (2014, 2016) result —
that MOND's *simple* interpolating function is excluded by Cassini-era Saturn ranging
by orders of magnitude, while the exponential (MLS) form survives — has never been
applied to AeST specifically. AeST's own papers discuss solar-system suppression only
qualitatively (a remark that Mercury-orbit corrections must be suppressed via
"screening or tracking," no quantitative bound derived). **This is exactly the test
cdot-4/T22 already ran once, for the project's own earlier closure** (§A.2 above): the
simple RAR closure gave a $\sim2800\times$ ephemeris exclusion there, mirroring Hees et
al. exactly. Recommend adding this as an explicit, near-term WP6 sub-task — the project
already has the numerical machinery (`T22`'s calculation, adaptable) to check whichever
specific interpolating-function shape AeST's quasistatic limit settles into, and it is
cheap relative to WP6's other items (PPN $\alpha_1,\alpha_2$; binary pulsars).

### B.3 The spin-1 no-go — resolved by inheritance, not merely avoided

cdot-4/T22 §3 flagged, and never resolved, that *"a fundamental Lorentz-invariant
massless vector field sourced by mass is otherwise forbidden (spin-1 no-go — like-source
repulsion, or negative field energy if the sign is flipped). The connecton route would
have to escape via its kinetic-medium character; locating this evasion is the central
open theoretical task."* This WP0 pass finds the exact evasion, worked out in the
foundational literature AeST is built on:

- **Carroll, Dulaney, Gresham & Tam, PRD 79, 065011 (2009), arXiv:0812.1049**: generic
  fixed-norm "aether" vector theories are "plagued either by ghosts or by tachyons" for
  essentially any kinetic term; a potential-minimized (Higgs-like) timelike vector
  *necessarily* develops a tachyonic ghost.
- **Zlosnik, Ferreira & Skordis, PRD 79, 065012 (2009), arXiv:0812.1050** (companion
  paper, same issue): the **sigma-model** kinetic term, with the vector's unit-timelike
  norm fixed by a **Lagrange-multiplier constraint** rather than potential minimization,
  is the unique fixed-norm construction with a Hamiltonian bounded from below in every
  Lorentz frame.

AeST's aether is built directly on this second result (Skordis is a co-author of both
the 2009 solution and the 2021 AeST paper). **The resolution is not "a massless vector
sourced by mass turns out to be fine after all" — it is that the naive construction is
abandoned in favor of a structurally different, norm-constrained object**, which is not
a force-mediating gauge field in the sense the T22-era no-go concerns. No source found
frames this explicitly as "the answer to the spin-1 no-go" in those words, so this
should be reported as this session's own synthesis of two independent results, not a
literature claim quoted verbatim — but the technical content is a direct, load-bearing
match. **This is a genuine, positive cross-check on the chassis choice**: cdot-8 is not
merely borrowing AeST's machinery, it is inheriting an already-solved version of a
problem this project got stuck on and left as its "central open theoretical task" in
2026-07 of cdot-4. Recommend adding this citation pair to Proposal §3's "structural
resonances," alongside M1/M2.

### B.4 Fallback chassis health check — the kill condition's escape route reassessed

The proposal's WP0 kill condition reads: *"a published fatal pathology of the AeST
chassis with no viable variant → re-scope to khronometric/BIMOND alternatives before
proceeding."* Both named fallbacks were checked for current health, since a kill
condition's value depends on the fallback actually being viable:

- **Khronometric/khronon-tensor gravity is a healthier, more active fallback than the
  phrasing suggests — and is not simply a fallback.** Blanchet & Skordis (JCAP 11(2024)040,
  arXiv:2404.06584; and arXiv:2507.00912, July 2025) have an actively-developed
  khronon-tensor MOND completion, **co-authored by Skordis himself**, explicitly claiming
  CMB and matter-power-spectrum agreement "similar to AeST." This is a **sibling
  chassis under joint development by the same group**, not an untested alternative to
  reach for only after a kill — worth naming explicitly rather than folding into a
  generic "khronometric" label. Open issues are comparable in character to AeST's own
  (a superhorizon Hamiltonian-boundedness caveat, argued but not proven benign; an
  order-unity khronon correction to MOND for non-stationary low-acceleration systems,
  per Flanagan 2023, arXiv:2302.14846).
- **BIMOND is markedly weaker than the proposal's phrasing implies.** Milgrom's own most
  recent paper (arXiv:2512.09353, Dec 2025) states outright: *"I cannot yet present a
  consistent model that complies with all the observations in cosmology."* No complete
  cosmological solution exists; ghost and Ostrogradsky-instability questions are named
  by Milgrom himself as open, unresolved matters of principle; the program is
  essentially single-author. **Recommend revising the kill-condition text**: the
  meaningful fallback is the Blanchet–Skordis khronon-tensor line, with BIMOND recorded
  as a distant, currently-incomplete second option — not co-equal with khronometric as
  the sentence currently implies.

### B.5 External criticism — one substantive, independent source

**Duerr & Wolf, "Methodological Reflections on the MOND/Dark Matter Debate,"
arXiv:2306.13026 (Studies in Hist. Phil. Sci.)** — philosophers of physics, fully
independent of the Skordis/Złośnik group — argue AeST (and relativistic MOND generally)
is "severely ad hoc": free-function/parameter proliferation, and specifically that the
free function is engineered to reproduce a dust-like ($\propto(1+z)^3$) scalar
energy-density scaling **with no independent motivation beyond making the CMB fit
work**. This is, read carefully, an outside, independent confirmation of exactly the
concern the proposal's own §3 "critical divergence" already names as the largest single
risk (AeST's cosmological success rests on a dust-like mimicker scalar that cdot-8 must
discard) — useful corroboration that this is a real, recognized weakness of the chassis
in its native form, not merely an internal worry. Separately, informal skepticism from
dark-matter-camp cosmologists (D. Spergel: "baroque"; K. Freese: "a lot of ingredients,"
*Physics* 14, 143 (2021)) is on record but adds no new technical content beyond the
standard "relabeled dark matter" objection.

**A related, sharpening finding**: **AeST's claimed CMB/matter-power-spectrum agreement
is, as of this search, still only the qualitative demonstration in the original 2021
PRL** — no 2024–2026 paper performs a quantitative ($\chi^2$/MCMC) fit against Planck
data. This does not change cdot-8's plan (§3 already commits to discarding AeST's
cosmological branch wholesale, so its CMB claim was never going to transfer regardless),
but it means the risk register's phrase "AeST's CMB success" should be read as "AeST's
qualitative CMB agreement" throughout — a strictly weaker claim than the proposal's
prose currently implies, and one more instance of the cdot-6-flagged caution (§A.1)
about not trusting a claimed cosmological success further than it has actually been
checked, applied here to AeST's own literature rather than an amateur preprint.

## Part C — Kill/no-kill verdict and carry-forward recommendations

**WP0's stated kill condition — a published fatal pathology of the AeST chassis with no
viable variant — is NOT triggered.** Every issue found is either (a) explicitly
non-fatal and under active investigation by the theory's own authors and independent
groups, or (b) a gap (binary pulsars; Cassini-level solar-system test) rather than a
positive exclusion. **Recommendation: proceed to WP1**, carrying forward:

1. Add the five-stage "two dictionaries" recurrence table (Part A) to Proposal §1.
2. Add an explicit anti-pattern commitment ("a derivation of $\lambda$ must exclude
   other candidate values, not merely reproduce $0.3056$") to K6/M2.
3. Add an Etherington-reciprocity check to WP1's validation plan for M3.
4. Add a Cassini/ephemeris-level interpolating-function check to WP6, adapting T22's
   own prior calculation — flagged as cheap and potentially fast-decisive.
5. Add the Carroll-Dulaney-Gresham-Tam/Zlosnik-Ferreira-Skordis citation pair to §3's
   structural resonances, as a positive cross-check (the spin-1 no-go from cdot-4/T22
   is inherited-solved, not merely sidestepped).
6. Revise the WP0 kill-condition fallback text: name the Blanchet–Skordis khronon-tensor
   line as the primary fallback (a sibling program, not merely an alternative), and
   demote BIMOND given Milgrom's own Dec 2025 admission of an incomplete cosmology.
7. Note, wherever "AeST's CMB success" is invoked (§3's critical-divergence discussion,
   the risk register), that this is presently a qualitative agreement, not a fitted
   result — consistent with, and reinforcing, §3's own decision to discard it regardless.

None of these six items change cdot-8's charter, design constraints, or work-package
order — they sharpen citations, add two cheap validation tools, and correct the
relative-health estimate of the two named fallback theories. Per this project's
consolidation convention, these are recommendations for the next merge into
`Proposal.md`, not applied here directly.
