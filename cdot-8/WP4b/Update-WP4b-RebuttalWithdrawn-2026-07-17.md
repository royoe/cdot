## 1. The category error, confirmed against this program's own established framework

`u_hat_of_a(a)` is, by construction, only the matter+radiation **census**
content — it explicitly excludes the scalar/dark-energy-like sector (the
"invoice," $\Omega_s$) that has been the centerpiece of this program since
the WP3 quadrature. The established relation, unchanged since those early
rounds, is $E(z)^2=u_\text{hat}(z)+\Omega_s(z)$, with $\Omega_\text{closure}
=0.074$ today and the scalar sector supplying the remaining $\approx0.926$
to reach $E^2(0)=1$. **$E(0)/\sqrt{u_\text{hat}(0)}=1/\sqrt{0.074}=3.67$ is
therefore correct, expected physics — not a bug.** My rebuttal's demand
that this ratio equal $1$ at $z=0$ asserted that the census alone should
reproduce $H_0$, i.e. it required the scalar sector not to exist. That is
a category error, and it is mine.

At BBN redshifts, $\Omega_s$ is small (established elsewhere in this
program as negligible to few-percent at these epochs), so $E^2\approx
u_\text{hat}$ there and $E/\sqrt{u_\text{hat}}\approx1$ with small
deviations — exactly the $0.965$–$1.02$ pattern the re-derivation reported.
This is not a coincidence rescued after the fact; it follows directly from
$\Omega_s$'s own established smallness at these epochs, checked independently
here rather than taken on the advisory's word.

skipped past on the way to conceding.
