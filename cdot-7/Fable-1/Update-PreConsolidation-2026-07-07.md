## 1. The Time-Dilation Caveat, Closed

Entry 4 quoted the SN spectral-aging constraint from memory (Blondin et al. 2008,
$b\approx0.97\pm0.10$) and flagged it for verification. Verified, and superseded by a
far stronger measurement: the Dark Energy Survey (White et al. 2024, MNRAS 533, 3365)
measures light-curve widths of 1504 SNe Ia over $0.1\lesssim z\lesssim1.2$,
parametrized as $\Delta t_\text{obs}=\Delta t_\text{em}(1+z)^b$, finding
$$b=1.003\pm0.005\,(\text{stat})\pm0.010\,(\text{sys}),$$
described by the authors as excluding non-time-dilating cosmologies at very high
significance. Consequences for this framework:
- The corrected photon sector predicts $b=1$ **exactly and generically in $s$**
  (Entry 4 §3) — consistent at $0.3\sigma$.
- The superseded conserved-frequency law predicted $b=3/5$ — now excluded at
  $\sim36\sigma$ rather than the $\sim4\sigma$ quoted from Blondin. Entry 4's
  correction was not merely mandatory; it was mandatory by a wide margin.
- Blondin et al. (2008) is confirmed as one of the earlier spectral-aging
  measurements in this literature; the DES number should replace it as the quoted
  constraint at merge.

## 2. The Thermal Sector — Derived, Three More Passes

The photon-sector machinery (conserved $k$, conserved mode occupation, adiabatic
evolution) determines the behavior of thermal radiation with no new assumptions.

**Blackbody form is preserved exactly.** A Planck distribution at emission,
$n(k)=[\exp(\hbar c_ek/k_BT_e)-1]^{-1}$, keeps its occupation per mode; since every
mode's frequency scales identically ($\omega=c(t)k$), the spectrum remains Planckian
at every later epoch with coordinate temperature $T(t)=T_e\,c(t)/c_e$. **No spectral
distortion is generated by propagation** — consistent with the FIRAS blackbody at the
$10^{-5}$ level, a test that kills most non-expanding photon sectors.

**The temperature–redshift relation.** In local units (temperature unit tracks the
energy unit $\propto c^{5/2}$):
$$\hat T\propto c^{-3/2}\quad\Longrightarrow\quad \hat T(z)=\hat T_0\,(1+z),$$
exactly the standard relation, as measured via SZ clusters and molecular absorbers
out to $z\sim3$. The same picture as redshift itself: the radiation doesn't cool in
coordinate terms — the local energy scale outgrows it.

**Number and energy densities.** Static space fixes coordinate mode density; the
local volume unit is $\propto c^{-9/2}$, so
$$\hat n_\gamma(z)=\hat n_{\gamma,0}(1+z)^3,\qquad
\hat u_\gamma(z)=\hat u_{\gamma,0}(1+z)^4,$$
matching the standard dilution laws and consistent with $\hat u=\hat a\hat T^4$ with
an invariant radiation constant (as Planck-unit invariance requires). Together with
Entry 4's Tolman result, the entire background thermal phenomenology of an expanding
universe is reproduced by the static, shrinking-ruler description.

## 3. New Flag: the Closure Has No Radiation Era

The Machian source in premise 2 counts **rest mass only**
($M_h=\frac43\pi R_h^3nm(t)$). But the radiation-to-matter energy ratio grows into
the past: $\hat u_\gamma/\hat\rho_m\hat c^2\propto(1+z)$, exactly as in standard
cosmology. At sufficiently early epochs (the analog of $z_\text{eq}\sim3400$)
radiation energy dominates the horizon's content, and the closure as written sources
$c(t)$ from the subdominant component. Consequences:

  questions since photon energy is epoch-dependent in coordinate units).
