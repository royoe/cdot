## Entry — AQUAL momentum question: premise inverted, no tension; two new correspondence checks

**Prompt (verbatim):**
> In AQUAL momentum in not conserved, but we have shown that it is in the cdot cosmology. Is there a tension or an easy explanation?

**Summary:** Easy explanation — the premise is historically inverted. Milgrom's
*original 1983* algebraic MOND ($\mu(g/a_0)g=g_N$ pointwise / per-particle modified
inertia) is the formulation that violates momentum conservation (unequal
action–reaction between unequal masses; isolated binaries self-accelerate).
**AQUAL was built by Bekenstein & Milgrom in 1984 precisely to repair this**: as a
translation-invariant Lagrangian field theory it conserves total (matter + field)
momentum exactly, with the proven vanishing of the total self-force on isolated
systems. Premise 4's "AQUAL-spirit" was chosen *for* the conservation laws. Likely
source of the confusion: pairwise Newton's third law does fail in AQUAL (nonlinear
field, no superposition) even though total momentum is exact — the two statements
get conflated in the literature.

The cdot modifications do not spoil it: $a_0(t)=\lambda\dot c(t)$ and
$m(t)\propto c^{1/2}$ are spatially uniform, so they break only time-translation
invariance (energy non-conserved at $O(H)$, as already recorded) while momentum and
angular momentum remain exact. Noted also that the cosmological closure uses only
the spherical relation, on which AQUAL/QUMOND/algebraic MOND coincide — the field
theory's conservation structure matters only in the local non-spherical sector,
where it was invoked.

**Two new checks derived from the question:** (1) free massive particles conserve
$p=m(t)v$ (the $m(t)$ dependence breaks energy, not momentum), giving
$\hat v_\text{pec}\propto(1+z)^{-1}$ — exactly the standard peculiar-velocity decay
$v\propto1/a$; (2) hence decoupled gas cools as $\hat T_\text{gas}\propto(1+z)^2$
vs photons' $(1+z)$ — the standard adiabatic pair, reproduced with no new
assumptions. Together with the photon sector ($\hbar k$ conservation = the redshift
law), these support a unifying statement for the merged Foundation: *nothing
propagating ever loses momentum in this framework — observers' units outgrow it.*
