# Brouwer et al. (2021) — "The Weak Lensing Radial Acceleration Relation: Constraining Modified Gravity and Cold Dark Matter theories with KiDS-1000"

**Citation:** M. M. Brouwer et al., A&A 650, A113 (2021); arXiv:2106.11677.
**Source stored:** `arXiv.2106.11677/RAR_paper_Brouwer_4.tex` (+ `.bbl`, `aa.cls`, `Figures/`).

## What this is, for cdot-8

One of WP5's two literature anchors for the lensing-RAR-vs-lens-redshift
confrontation (the other is Mistele et al. 2024, arXiv:2310.15248).
KiDS-1000 galaxy-galaxy weak lensing, RAR construction and comparison to
MOND/CDM predictions.

## Aspects load-bearing for cdot-8

- **Lens sample**: KiDS-bright, $0.1<z<0.5$, mean $\langle z\rangle
  \approx0.2$–$0.25$ — **pooled as a single population, not binned by
  redshift**. This was the key reconnaissance finding of WP5 §8: neither
  this paper nor Mistele et al. 2024 provides the redshift-binned lensing
  RAR the proposal's "lensing-RAR-by-lens-redshift" deliverable would
  need to compare against directly.
- Compares to McGaugh's canonical $a_0=1.2\times10^{-10}$ m/s² as an
  external baseline rather than reporting an independent fitted value of
  its own for $a_0$.
- **$6\sigma$ early-vs-late-type RAR split** (by Sérsic index and $u-r$
  colour) — confirmed verbatim; a named, real differential systematic any
  future redshift-binned reanalysis (the flagged WP5b/external-proposal
  scope) would need to control for.
- **Missing-baryons discussion** (§4.3, BAHAMAS comparison): real and
  discussed at length, but — **checked three times against this specific
  primary source, including the publisher's own aanda.org page** — does
  **not** contain the phrase "the single most severe limitation of our
  analysis" or equivalent severity language attributed to it by one
  advisory round. The paper's own text leans the other way ("current
  observational constraints indicate that the resulting corrections are
  likely moderate"). This is a **standing, held correction** in the WP5/
  WP6 record — the underlying point (missing baryons is a real,
  discussed, common-mode systematic) is unaffected, but the specific
  quote should not be repeated as this paper's own words.
