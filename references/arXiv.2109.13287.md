# Skordis & Złośnik (2022) — "Aether scalar tensor theory: Linear stability on Minkowski space"

**Citation:** C. Skordis & T. Złośnik, Phys. Rev. D 106, 104041 (2022); arXiv:2109.13287.
**Source stored:** `arXiv.2109.13287/AeST_MinkLinStab.tex` (+ `.bbl`, bib styles).

## What this is, for cdot-8

The **companion stability paper** to the AeST founding paper — works out
AeST's perturbation spectrum (scalar, vector, tensor) around a Minkowski
background, the condensate mass, and the stability conditions. This
became the single most load-bearing external source for **WP5's**
weak-field/lensing confrontation and **WP6's** tensor-speed and PPN work.

## Aspects load-bearing for cdot-8

- **Eq. 1** — the full quasistatic action, $S=\int d^4x\sqrt{-g}/(16\pi
  \tilde G)\{R-2\Lambda-\frac{K_B}2F^{\mu\nu}F_{\mu\nu}+2(2-K_B)J^\mu
  \nabla_\mu\phi-(2-K_B)\mathcal Y-\mathcal F(\mathcal Y,\mathcal Q)-
  \lambda(A^\mu A_\mu+1)\}+S_m$. Confirmed the **same prefactor and sign**
  as WP3's independently-validated minisuperspace term
  $-\frac{a^3N}{16\pi\tilde G}F(Q)$ — the exact match WP5's $m_\text{eff}$
  dictionary was conditional on.
- **Exact covariant definitions**: $\mathcal Q=A^\mu\nabla_\mu\phi$,
  $\mathcal Y=(g^{\mu\nu}+A^\mu A^\nu)\nabla_\mu\phi\nabla_\nu\phi$,
  $J^\mu=A^\nu\nabla_\nu A^\mu$ (the aether's own **acceleration**
  vector — corrected in WP6 after an earlier looser recollection).
- **Eq. 10**: the free-function expansion around the condensate point,
  $\mathcal F=(2-K_B)\lambda_s\mathcal Y-2\mathcal K_2(\mathcal Q-
  \mathcal Q_0)^2+\ldots$ — the basis of WP5's $m_\text{eff}$ derivation.
- **Eq. 58**: the condensate mass $\mu^2\equiv2\mathcal K_2\mathcal
  Q_0^2/(2-K_B)$ — *the* $\mu$ that Mistele-McGaugh-Hossenfelder 2023
  constrain. Confirmed verbatim, not paraphrased.
- **Eq. 31**: the stability condition $\mathcal K_2>0$ — used to confirm
  cdot-8's $F_{QQ}<0$ sign is exactly the sign stability requires, not a
  tachyon warning.
- **Eq. 20**: the tensor-mode action, shown to be *exactly* GR's
  ("consistent with the earlier, more general calculation that tensor
  modes in the superclass of theories... propagate at the speed of
  light," citing Skordis-Złośnik 2019) — the primary-source basis for
  WP6's tensor-speed discharge.
- **Quadratic-order sector additivity**: "the MOND-type term $\sim
  |\mathcal Y|^{3/2}$... do[es] not contribute to the second order
  action" — the basis for WP5's claim that cdot-8's $Q$-sector
  modification cannot leak into the $\mathcal Y$-sector (lensing/MOND
  shape), later sharpened in WP6 (the same fact underlies why the aether
  alone has no propagating spin-0 mode).
- Discussion of "screening and tracking" as AeST's two GR-restoration
  mechanisms ($\lambda_s$, higher-$\mathcal Y$ terms) — mechanism class
  only, no unique functional form or quantitative solar-system bound
  given (confirmed absence, used in WP6 sub-task 1's reframing).
- States "$k_+\lesssim$ Mpc$^{-1}$" as the only quantitative window
  discussed for low-momentum instabilities — no Cassini/PPN numbers.
