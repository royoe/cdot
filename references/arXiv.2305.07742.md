# Mistele (2023/2024) — "A new scale in the quasi-static limit of Aether Scalar Tensor Theory"

**Citation:** D. Mistele, Phys. Rev. D 110, 024062 (2024); arXiv:2305.07742.
**Source stored:** `arXiv.2305.07742/aest-static.tex` (+ `.bbl`, `plots/`).

## What this is, for cdot-8

Identifies and separates **two distinct mass/scale parameters** hiding in
AeST's quasistatic action — the central subject of WP5's $m_\times$
episode (a bundling error, caught and corrected mid-session) and the
launch point for WP5's $m_\text{eff}$ investigation.

## Aspects load-bearing for cdot-8

- **Eq. 1/21**: $m_\times\equiv Q_0\sqrt{(2-K_B)/(2K_B)}$ — built
  *entirely* from $K_B$ (aether kinetic coefficient) and the fixed
  coupling constant $Q_0$, with **no dependence on the free function $J$**
  (cdot-8's $F(Q)$ analog). Confirmed verbatim: "$m_\times$... is not
  related to the ghost condensate... does not affect the ability of
  AeST to reproduce MOND." This is why cdot-8's charter modification
  (which lives entirely in the free function) cannot remove the
  $m_\times$ two-quasistatic-limit ambiguity — but also why $m_\times$ is
  irrelevant to the actual weak-lensing tension (see below).
- **The separate parameter $m$** (the ghost-condensate mass): "$m$
  controls whether AeST reproduces MOND" — *this*, not $m_\times$, is
  the parameter Mistele, McGaugh & Hossenfelder (2023, A&A 676, A100)
  constrain with weak-lensing data. Session error #6 (both mine and the
  advisor's) was bundling these two scales under one hypothesis before
  this distinction was checked against the primary text.
- States $m_\times$ "is, to the best of our knowledge, not yet
  constrained phenomenologically" (§V) and has sub-percent effects on
  galaxy-scale rotation curves — the basis for WP5's decision that the
  two-quasistatic-limit question is a wide-binary footnote, not a WP5
  blocker, and that the lensing-RAR curve is unblocked regardless of
  which limit applies.
- **Notational caution**: this paper's own $Q_0$ is a fixed action
  constant of dimension 1 — checked (and initially over-corrected, then
  reconciled via the companion stability paper, arXiv:2109.13287) against
  cdot-8's own evolving background invariant $Q(t)/Q_0(t)$.
- Quotes AeST's full quasistatic action (Eq. 1), reproduced and
  cross-checked against WP3's independently-validated minisuperspace term.
