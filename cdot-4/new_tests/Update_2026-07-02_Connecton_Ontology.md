*Session type: constructive. Question posed: with the pilot-wave framing discarded, what
is the most coherent presentation of what a connecton actually is — given that it moves

results exclude both.** A previously-unflagged consistency tension (the cascade

connecton is not a field quantum but a **unit of connection — an entanglement link
(bit thread) between matter and the horizon.** This turns out to be (a) the author's
own original definition, predating the pilot-wave detour; (b) an exact quantitative
match to T14's holographic-saturation density; and (c) the substrate of which photons
and gravitons are derived collective excitations — which is why all three share the
speed c.*

3. **Number-conserved**; absorbed/re-emitted by matter via virtual e⁺e⁻ pairs;

4. Momentum-negligible individually; the sea cannot push (Inertia No-Go; the 26-order

6. Bosonic indistinguishability (load-bearing in the RAR closure, T14).

9. Two-fluid structure: a diffusive short-scale fraction (the Poisson δn = the

10. Relational: the global count sets c (premise 2); local structure is gravity (T14).

## 2. The Forcing Argument: the Connecton Cannot Obey E = ℏω

A new consistency finding (verified): constraints 1, 7, and 8 are **jointly
inconsistent with the connecton being a standard quantum.** The derived cascade
spectrum puts equal connecton *number* in each of ~89 wavelength e-folds
(~4×10⁴⁰ m⁻³ per e-fold). If each carried the standard quantum energy E = ℏck of its
wavelength, the energy density would be:

| wavelength decade | energy density if E = ℏck | vs ρ_Λ |
|---|---|---|
| λ_C (UV end of cascade) | 3×10²⁷ J/m³ | 10³⁷× over |
| 1 mm (CMB band) | 8×10¹⁸ J/m³ | 10²⁸× over (and 10³³× the CMB itself) |
| 1 m | 8×10¹⁵ J/m³ | 10²⁵× over |

The holographic budget (every connecton at ~ℏH₀) is consistent with the spectrum only
if **the connecton's energy is decoupled from its wavelength** — its "wavelength" in
the cascade is a spatial extent/correlation length, not a de Broglie wavelength.
Photons and gravitons both rigidly obey E = ℏω. Therefore:

- **Not photonic**, additionally because: photon coupling ∝ charge (breaks EP —
  constraint 5); photon number is not conserved (breaks 3); a photon population in the
  mm decade at any relevant density is excluded by the CMB.
- **Not gravitonic**, additionally because: graviton number is not conserved (breaks
  3); and it would be circular — a graviton is a quantum *of* the metric, but in this
  model there is no prior metric: the sea's state (δn, w) *constitutes* the effective
  metric. The gravity quantum cannot be the substrate that builds gravity.

The intuition "moves at c ⇒ photonic or gravitonic" presumes the connecton is a
particle species. The constraints say it is one level below the particles.

## 3. The Coherent Presentation: One Connecton = One Unit of Matter–Horizon Connection

**Definition.** A connecton is a single unit of connection — an entanglement link, in
the bit-thread sense — between a local degree of freedom and the cosmological horizon
(or, after re-anchoring, between local degrees of freedom). It is a *relation*, not a
particle: it has endpoints, an extent, and a conserved identity, but no kinetic energy
of its own; its ℏH₀-scale energy is the thermodynamic cost of one unit of horizon
information.

**This is a return, not a novelty.** The author's original definition (2026-06-30
thread, verbatim kernel): *"a field particle that just represents a connection between
points."* The pilot-wave framing was the discarded *mechanism* bolted onto that
definition (its coherent-interference gravity failed quantitatively — one of T14's
five dead MOND routes); the connection kernel survived and is what the holographic
result now makes quantitative.

**The exact identity that clinches it (verified).** Freedman–Headrick bit threads —
the flow-line reformulation of holographic entanglement entropy — have a maximum
areal density of exactly one thread per 4L_p². Threads crossing the horizon at
saturation, filling the volume, give a number density
$$n_\text{threads} = \frac{A/4L_p^2}{V} = \frac{3}{4L_p^2 R_0} = 3.62\times10^{42}\ \text{m}^{-3}$$
— **numerically and structurally identical to T14's holographic-saturation premise
n_holo.** T14's "new premise" was, unknowingly, the bit-thread saturation condition.
The total count N_hor ≈ 8×10¹²³ is the de Sitter-entropy scale (~10⁴⁴ links per
baryon).

**How each constraint reads in link language:**

1. *Energy:* the per-link energy is the horizon-thermodynamic bit cost. Candidates
   (in units of ℏH₀): Landauer cost k_BT_dS ln2 = 0.110 (with T_dS = ℏH₀/2πk_B) or
   0.055 (with H^hor); T14's ℏc/R₀ = 0.167; T14's m_c = 0.5. **The audit's factor-3
   ambiguity is thereby recast as a thermodynamic normalization question with a
   derivation path** (compute the bit cost properly), not a free choice.
2. *Moves at c:* the link does not travel; re-anchorings, count changes, and
   disturbances propagate along it causally at the network's signal speed — which is
   what c *is* (T12). "Propagates at c" is a statement about link dynamics, and the
   diffusion mathematics (random-walking endpoints at c between re-anchorings) is
   unchanged.
3. *Conservation + emission:* re-anchoring conserves links; "continuous emission" is
   matter forming new links to the growing horizon — the cosmological count growth
   c ∝ N *is* the link-formation process. Cosmology and gravity are one bookkeeping.
4. *Momentum-neutrality:* relations carry no momentum; the force is the gradient of
   the link-count field (an entropic/energetic gradient), matching the Inertia No-Go
   and the ram budget exactly.
5. *Count coupling / EP:* links attach to degrees of freedom as such — per particle,
   not per charge or per binding energy. Composition-independence is definitional.
6. *Indistinguishability:* links between identical endpoint classes are
   indistinguishable — the RAR closure's premise, now grounded.
7–9. *Spectrum and two-fluid:* the cascade n(k) ∝ 1/k is a distribution of **link
   lengths**, not quantum energies (resolving §2's tension). Interaction happens at
   **endpoints only**, so the interaction probability per unit length ~ 1/L: short
   cascade links re-anchor constantly (diffusive — the Poisson δn), horizon-anchored
   links have no mid-body interactions (ballistic — the river). This supersedes the
   QC session's illustrative Rayleigh σ(λ) with a parameter-free, geometry-only split
   — the Item-6 residual is thereby retired.
10. *The river's carrier:* the collective frame of the long-link network — its
   drift can be arbitrarily slow while all endpoint dynamics run at c, resolving the
   QC session's "pressureless dust" carrier inconsistency in the natural way.

**Photons and gravitons, then:** both are *derived* — collective excitations of the
link network. The EM sector: ε₀, μ₀ as network polarizability (T12's existing claim),
photons as its propagating modes. The gravitational sector: δn and w as the network's
coarse-grained state, GR's metric (and gravitons, if quantized) as its effective
description. **All three move at c because c is the one update speed of the one
substrate — shared speed by shared substrate, not shared species.** This answers the
posed dichotomy at its root.
