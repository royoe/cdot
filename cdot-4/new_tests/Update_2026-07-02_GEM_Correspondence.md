## 1. Why GEM Fits the Model Better Than PV Ever Did

GEM is not a separate theory — it is the $(g_{00}, g_{0i})$ sector of linearized GR,
organized as Maxwell-like equations sourced by the mass-current four-vector
$(\rho, \mathbf{j}_m)$, with a gravitoelectric field $\mathbf{E}_g$ and gravitomagnetic
field $\mathbf{B}_g$ (carrying the spin-2 signature factor 4 relative to the naive EM
analogy). Structurally, the connecton program is already GEM-shaped in a way it was
never PV-shaped:

1. **Sourcing:** GEM couples to mass *currents*; the connecton sea is a
   momentum-carrying kinetic medium with exact momentum conservation (Noether, T11) —
   currents are its native language. A scalar index $K$ has no current coupling.
2. **The gravitomagnetic field already exists:** $B_c$ (T14/T17/T19) is exactly a
   gravitomagnetic-type object — sourced by coherent mass flow, acting via
   $v \times B_c$. The model has been doing GEM phenomenology at galactic scales for
   three topic documents.
3. **The gravitoelectric part is derived:** connecton diffusion → Newtonian $1/r$
   (T14) *is* the $\mathbf{E}_g$ sector.
4. **Wave sector for free:** collective modes of the sea propagate at the sea's signal
   speed $c$ (T12) → $c_\text{gw} = c$ automatically, satisfying GW170817's
   $|c_\text{gw}-c|/c < 10^{-15}$ (closes the premise-level check flagged in the Test
   Battery, item 8).

## 2. The Big Structural Win: α₂ and Emergent Lorentz Invariance

The PPN/PV session identified preferred-frame PPN $\alpha_2 < 4\times10^{-7}$ as the
sharpest unexamined threat: the solar system moves through the sea at ~370 km/s,
$(v/c)^2 \approx 1.5\times10^{-6}$ — raw scale an order of magnitude above the bound.
Scalar preferred-frame theories (PV included) generically fail this.

The GEM route offers a principled zero: **if the connecton sector's effective dynamics
are Maxwell-like and (emergently) Lorentz invariant at speed $c$, then uniform motion
through the sea is unobservable — $\mathbf{E}_g$ and $\mathbf{B}_g$ transform into each
other exactly as in electrodynamics, and $\alpha_1 = \alpha_2 = 0$ identically.** This
is the Lorentzian-ether structure: an ontological preferred frame with no kinematical
signature, because matter, rulers, clocks, *and the gravity sector itself* are all built
from the same medium with the same invariance group. The model already asserts exactly
this architecture for EM and matter (T12: $c$ as the sea's signal speed; dynamical
length contraction); the GEM proposal extends it to gravity. **Proposed premise-level
statement:** the connecton collective dynamics are Lorentz invariant with respect to
$c$; deviations, if any, are confined to the deep-MOND coherent-flow sector.
Consequence: $\alpha_1, \alpha_2 = 0$ at solar-system accelerations — converting the
program's sharpest threat into a structural requirement with a clear derivation target
(show the connecton Boltzmann/transport equations admit the emergent symmetry).

**The knife edge (stated honestly):** a *fundamental* Lorentz-invariant massless vector
field sourced by mass predicts (i) like-source repulsion and (ii) negative field energy
if the sign is flipped — the classic spin-1 no-go. GR escapes via spin-2. The connecton
route must escape via its kinetic-medium character (attraction is native to
flux-shadowing, not quantum exchange; the effective GEM fields ride on a large positive
background energy density, breaking the no-go's positivity mapping). Locating this
evasion precisely is the central theoretical task of the GEM route. Its empirical
fingerprint is quantitative:

- **Lense–Thirring normalization.** GR: node precession $2GJ/(c^2a^3(1-e^2)^{3/2})$ —
  30.7 mas/yr for LAGEOS, measured to ~2% (LARES); GP-B to ~19%. The connecton $B_c$ of
  a spinning mass must reproduce this, including the spin-2 factor 4 that a naive
  spin-1 analogy misses. Two-sided: deriving the 4 from connecton transport would be a
  landmark success; a factor-4 miss is a clean kill.
- **Quadrupole radiation coefficient.** Dipole radiation vanishes *automatically* even
  for vector-like coupling, because gravitational "charge" ≡ mass:
  $\dot{\mathbf d} = \sum m_i\mathbf v_i = \mathbf P = $ const $\Rightarrow
  \ddot{\mathbf d} = 0$. The first radiative channel is quadrupole — but the spin-1 and
  spin-2 quadrupole coefficients differ, and the double pulsar tests the GR value to
  $1.3\times10^{-4}$. The connecton radiation-reaction calculation is now a
  well-posed, decisive target (new open item; supersedes the vague "GW sector" note).

## 3. What GEM Cannot Do: the Clock/Light Sector Survives from the PV Session

A flat, static space with spatially uniform constants has **no gravitational redshift
at all**: static clocks at different heights are identical machines in identical local
conditions, and photon frequency is conserved in flight (premise 4). Pound–Rebka, GPS,
and Galileo-GREAT therefore *force* local constants to vary — i.e. the vacuum-index
sector $K(r)$ with the uniquely forced dictionary of the PPN/PV update ($A=2$,
$\sigma = 3/2$, all local energies $\propto K^{-1/2}$). GEM supplies forces on matter;
it cannot make a clock in a well run slow, nor supply the $g_{ij}$ half of light
bending. **The two correspondences are complementary blocks of the weak-field metric:**

| Metric block | Physical content | Model implementation | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| $g_{00}$ (force) | $\mathbf{E}_g$, Newtonian pull | connecton diffusion (T14) | derived |
| $g_{0i}$ | $\mathbf{B}_g$, frame dragging | $B_c$ (T14/T17/T19) | present; normalization untested |
| $g_{00}$ (clocks) | gravitational redshift | index $\delta K$ + local PV dictionary | forced by data (PPN/PV update) |
| $g_{ij}$ | rulers; light's second half | same $\delta K$ ($n = K$) | forced by data (PPN/PV update) |

**A new consistency lock this framing makes visible:** in GR, one potential ($g_{00}$)
does double duty for force and clocks. Here the force comes from the momentum-flux
gradient of the sea perturbation $\delta n_c$, and the clock shift from the index
response to the *same* $\delta n_c$. Their relative normalization is not automatic — it
is a constraint on connecton microphysics (one relation between the flux-to-potential
and density-to-index coefficients), enforced observationally by the consistency of
$GM_\oplus$ from orbits with $GM_\oplus$ from clock redshifts (GPS/ACES, high
precision). The GEM framing thus *organizes* the derivation targets: a single
$\delta n_c$ field must yield $\phi_g$ (flux), $\delta K = 2\phi_g/c^2$ (index), and
$\mathbf{B}_g$ (flow) with GR-locked ratios. This supersedes the bare "derive $A=2$"
item: it is now three locked coefficients from one microphysical object.

## 4. MAJOR NEW FINDING: the Closure's High-Acceleration Tail vs Planetary Ephemerides

Taking the solar-system domain seriously (which the GEM question forces) exposes a
confrontation that galaxy data cannot see. The derived closure
$g_x(g_x + g_\text{bar}) = g_\text{bar}\,g_\dagger$ (T14) — MOND's *simple*
interpolating function — has the asymptotic tail
$$g_x \to g_\dagger - g_\dagger^2/g_\text{bar} \quad (g_\text{bar} \gg g_\dagger),$$
i.e. a **constant residual acceleration $\approx 1.13\times10^{-10}$ m/s² toward the
Sun at every planet** (verified: $g_x = 1.135\times10^{-10}$ at Earth, Saturn, and
Neptune alike). Planetary-ephemeris bounds on anomalous constant accelerations at
Saturn (Cassini ranging era, INPOP-class) are $\sim 4\times10^{-14}$ m/s²: the tail is
**excluded by a factor ~2800 (~3.5 orders of magnitude)**. This is the known result
that Cassini/ephemeris analyses (Hees et al. 2014, 2016) exclude MOND's simple
interpolating function specifically — while the exponential MLS RAR function
($g_\text{obs} = g_\text{bar}/(1-e^{-\sqrt{g_\text{bar}/g_\dagger}})$) leaves an
utterly negligible residual at Saturn. Crucially, **the 0.020 dex galaxy-scale match
(T14/T15) cannot distinguish simple from MLS-exponential** — they agree over the
galactic data range and diverge only at $g_\text{bar}/g_\dagger \sim 10^5$–$10^8$,
exactly the solar-system regime. The closure's celebrated derivation is therefore
exposed at its high-acceleration end.

**Resolution paths (in decreasing order of attractiveness):**

1. **Exponentiate the tail from the mechanism.** The T14 derivation used
   indistinguishability of sourced vs background connectons with the marginally-bound
   population at $r_t$. The Sun's $r_t = \sqrt{GM_\odot/g_\dagger} \approx 7.2\times
   10^3$ AU (Oort-cloud scale); planets sit at $r/r_t \sim 10^{-3}$, deep inside, where
   the marginal population the mechanism relies on is arguably absent or exponentially
   rare. A refined derivation weighting the marginal population with its Boltzmann
   factor plausibly converts the algebraic tail into an exponential one — the concrete
   target is the MLS form, which the data (galactic *and* solar-system) actually
   prefer. This would upgrade the closure from "matches simple" to "derives the
   observed exponential cutoff," turning the exclusion into a discovery opportunity.
2. **Environmental saturation (EFE analog).** The Galactic field at the Sun is
   $2.15\times10^{-10}$ m/s² $= 1.9\,g_\dagger$ — already trans-critical. If the
   closure's anomalous component is set by the total local flux environment (already
   saturated by the Galaxy, and already accounted for in the Galaxy's own rotation),
   the Sun's increment is purely Newtonian. Whether the connecton mechanism composes
   fields this way (an external-field effect analog) is a well-posed transport
   question; note MOND field theories invoke exactly this escape, and Hees et al.
   still constrain it — path 1 and 2 likely must operate together.
3. **The honest stake:** if neither works, the pointwise closure is falsified in the
   high-acceleration regime and the T14 RAR result must be demoted to an emergent
   galactic-scale relation with an unexplained domain boundary.

This finding is independent of GEM-vs-PV framing — it applies to any local completion —
but it was invisible until the solar-system domain was engaged.

## 5. Two-Regime Gravitomagnetism (consistency note for T17/T19)

The galactic $B_c$ used in T17/T19 (Lorentz filter, disk spring) is *enormously*
stronger than GR's frame dragging from the same mass currents — it is a deep-MOND
coherent-flow object, not linearized-GR gravitomagnetism. The GEM route therefore
implies **two gravitomagnetic regimes mirroring the two-regime dictionary**: in the
Newtonian regime $B_c$ must reduce to GR's tiny $2GJ/c^2r^3$ dipole (LAGEOS-tested),
while in the trans-critical regime the coherent-flow enhancement operates. The same
transition machinery that must cut off $g_x$ (Part 4) must also govern $B_c$'s
regime change — a unification requirement, and a falsifiable one: any solar-system
enhancement of frame dragging above GR is excluded at 2%.
