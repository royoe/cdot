## 1. The Firm Core (derived, stable)

The model is a flat, coordinate-static cosmology in which all dynamics are carried by a
time-varying speed of light set relationally by the matter content ($c\propto N$, the
volume law $c\propto R^3$). From four premises plus the EM-forced vacuum scaling
$\epsilon_0\propto\mu_0\propto c^{-1}$, the following results are rigorous:

- **The squared redshift law**, $1+z=(c_\text{now}/c_\text{emit})^2$, with the deep
  structural identity that the redshift power and the mass-scaling exponent are the
  *same* degree of freedom ($P=s+2$): the supernova Hubble diagram directly measures the
  invariant-mass premise.
- **The two Hubble constants**, $H_0^{\text{obs}} = P\,H_0^{\text{hor}}$ — an essential
  bookkeeping discovery whose earlier neglect produced a factor-2 age error and a
  spurious −1.8 mag SN offset. Horizon radius $R_0 = 6c_0/H_0^{\text{obs}} \approx 25.7$
  Gpc.
- **A finite proper age of ~21 Gyr** ($\tau_\infty = 3/(2H_0^{\text{obs}})$) despite an
  infinite coordinate past — no Big Bang in map time, and no stellar-age tension.
  Lookback times match ΛCDM to ~1% at $z\le1$ and exceed it beyond $z\approx2$ (extra
  time for early structure, JWST-relevant).
- **Structural deceleration**: $q_0 = 1/(nP) = +1/6 > 0$ for any power-law horizon. The
  model *cannot* mimic dark energy for any parameter choice. This converts the theory
  into a sharply falsifiable rival: it lives or dies on whether cosmic acceleration is
  real and standard-candle-clean.
- **Conservation flip** (Noether, from the static map's symmetries): momentum and angular
  momentum exactly conserved; energy exactly not. Photons conserve frequency; massive
  particles conserve momentum; peculiar velocities damp as $(1+z)^{1/2}$ relative to $c$.
- **Classical $\alpha$-invariance** — forced, not tuned: $e,\hbar$ invariant and
  $\epsilon_0\propto c^{-1}$ cancel identically, consistent with ppm-level quasar bounds.

## 2. The Great Pivot: Invariant G (cdot-4)

The single most consequential empirical event in the project. The legacy PV-motivated
choice $G\propto c^{-2}$ was decisively refuted by a self-consistent Lunar Laser Ranging
computation (the clock-rate and ranging effects *add* to the orbital-expansion signal):
predicted non-tidal range rate +41.4 mm/yr vs a bound of 0.058 mm/yr — a **×720**
exclusion. Adopting invariant $G$ resolved LLR, pulsar timing, and the BBN
$G$-amplification at root, but forced a cascade of honest withdrawals: orbital expansion
($r\propto c^2$), galaxy-size evolution $r_e\propto(1+z)^{-1}$ (T10, fully withdrawn),
and the original constant-flux mechanism. The theoretical debt (why $G\propto c^0$?) is
explicitly carried, not hidden. This episode is the project's best demonstration of its
own methodology: quantitative self-refutation followed by systematic propagation.

most mature example of prediction → data confrontation → systematics-aware retreat

## 5. Live Threats (honest ledger)

1. **Supernova Hubble diagram (T4) — the most serious.** A direct Pantheon+ fit (1371
   Hubble-flow SNe) gives $\Delta\chi^2 = +195$ vs ΛCDM on diagonal errors. The model's
   only escape is the Chandrasekhar-candle systematic ($M_\text{Ch}\propto c^{3/2}$,
   robust bound $E_\text{total}\propto c^{7/2}$): its **sign is favourable but its raw
   magnitude massively overshoots**; everything hinges on the un-resolved ejecta-velocity
   exponent $q$ (Phillips-absorption window $q\approx3.3$–3.8 vs the self-consistent
   $q=-1$). Full-covariance refit and a $q$-resolving radiation-hydro treatment are the
   gating tasks.
2. **QED running of $\alpha$ (T7).** A naive estimate gives ~500 ppm drift at $z\sim1$ vs
   ESPRESSO's 1–2 ppm bound — potentially a clean exclusion; the careful calculation has
   never been completed. Highest-leverage unfinished theory calculation in the project.
3. **The CMB (T16).** The hardest constraint. Genuine progress: the first acoustic peak
   translates via self-similarity ($R = 3\rho_b/4\rho_\gamma$ is $c$-invariant) with no
   dark matter needed. Higher peaks require clustered pressureless wells → the PBH
   genesis program, gated on the single unproven "relic freeze-out at $r_s/R\sim1$"
   claim (Reading 2). Status: "not yet dead," not "in agreement."
4. **PBH-vs-RAR double counting (new, this audit).** The derived baryon-only RAR and
   halo-scale PBH dark matter at $\Omega\sim0.25$ cannot both be true as stated; the
   division of labour must be quantified (see Update §III.1).
